User Guide

Traffic

The Traffic page records this machine's daily up and down traffic through the core, so you can review usage trends over time.

What this page solves

It answers "how much traffic did I use these days". All data comes from the local core — no server collects it; what you see is this computer's actual usage.

Choosing a time range

The top of the page switches between four time ranges:

  • Last 7 days — usage over the past week.
  • Last 30 days — usage over the past month.
  • Last 90 days — usage over the past three months.
  • All — every record in history.

After switching, the chart and table below update in sync. Shorter ranges show clearer trends; longer ranges are better for overall scale. "Last 7 days" is enough for daily checks — reach for "Last 30 days" or "Last 90 days" to see long-term usage. Switching only affects the display; no historical data is deleted.

What to look at and how

The page presents data two ways at once — a trend chart and a detail table: the chart shows the curve's ups and downs so you can spot high-traffic days at a glance; the table gives each day's exact numbers. The table has four columns:

ColumnMeaning
DateThe day this row covers.
DownloadTotal traffic received that day.
UploadTotal traffic sent that day.
TotalCombined up and down traffic for the day.

If the current range has no records yet, the page shows "No records in this range". Widen the range, or come back after using the app for a while.

Traffic by app

At the bottom of the page there is an App traffic panel that groups traffic by originating process, showing which app used the most bandwidth over the period. The table has five columns — app, download, upload, total and last active — sorted by total, highest first.

Data is sampled from connection records every 15 seconds while the core runs; only connections with an identifiable process are counted. The list may be empty right after start-up and fills in shortly. Totals persist across restarts. To start over, click Reset at the panel's top-right to clear all per-app totals; daily records are not affected.

Traffic by node

Below the App traffic panel there is a Node traffic panel that groups traffic by the exit node each connection finally leaves through, with direct traffic listed separately as a DIRECT row — handy for comparing how much went through the proxy and how much went direct. The table has five columns — node, download, upload, total and last active — sorted by total, highest first.

Data is likewise sampled every 15 seconds while the core runs and accumulated; totals persist across restarts. To start over, click Reset at the panel's top-right to clear all per-node totals; daily records and App traffic are not affected.

Where the data is stored

Data is recorded continuously while the client runs and stored in the data directory: daily records in traffic_history.json, per-app traffic in app_traffic.json and per-node traffic in node_traffic.json. Therefore history is kept after a restart and is not lost when you exit. All these files are maintained automatically by the client — no manual editing needed.

Only traffic through the core is counted

Only traffic that goes through the core is counted. Direct connections that bypass the core aren't included, so the totals may be lower than the adapter's actual usage.

About clearing records

Daily records have no dedicated clear button — you can overwrite them indirectly by importing a backup under Settings → Maintenance → Backup & restore. Per-app and per-node totals can be cleared with Reset at each panel's top-right.
